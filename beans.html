Questions about our beans

Can you ship a bag to me?

We have shipped our blends to customers as far as New York and Denver. Send us a message with your city and we will tell you how to order.

Do you have decaf or a darker roast?

Yes. Along with the house blend we pour a bold dark roast and a smooth decaf. Ask at the counter which are in stock as bags.

Who roasts your coffee?

We work with Split Oak Coffee Roasters on our custom blends, and the house blend is air roasted every week.

A St. Cloud family and a love of craft coffee

Madilynns is run by a husband and wife from a sixth-generation Osceola County family. The shop carries the name of the next generation.

How it started

Inspired by independent coffee shops across the country

The idea took shape in the summer of 2018, after visiting independent coffee shops across the country. On June 27, 2019 we opened our doors on 10th Street.

In September 2022 the shop became Madilynns Craft Coffee Shop, named for our daughter, Madilynn.

Our goal is always to bring our passion and love for craft coffee to the people of our community!

Kristen Lanier, co-owner

Along the way

How we got here

  • Summer 2018

    The idea for a craft coffee shop in St. Cloud takes shape.

  • June 27, 2019

    The doors open at 1022 10th Street in downtown St. Cloud.

  • September 2022

    The shop takes the name Madilynns Craft Coffee Shop.

  • Today

    Our coffee is also poured at The Inn at Celebration, and our blends ship as far as New York and Denver.

The coffee

Coffee forward, with flavor to follow

Our house blend is a two-origin mix of Ethiopian and Brazilian beans. We build our blends with Split Oak Coffee Roasters and roast fresh every week. Every drink is made to order, from a simple drip coffee to the Pink Drink with fresh strawberries.

The shop shares its space with a family-owned hair salon.

Come to socialize, to work, or to grab a coffee on your way. We hope you feel right at home.

Questions about the menu

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Order for pickup and your drink will be ready at the counter. You can also order through Uber Eats or DoorDash.

What coffee do you use?

Our house blend is a two-origin mix of Ethiopian and Brazilian beans, roasted fresh every week with Split Oak Coffee Roasters.

Can I get my latte hot instead of iced?

Most of our specialty lattes come both ways. Hot specialty lattes are $5.79 and iced are $5.89.

Do you have drinks without caffeine?

Hibiscus Zest is caffeine free and sugar free. The kids drinks, lemonades and the Very Berry Smoothie have no coffee.
